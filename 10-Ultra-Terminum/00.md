abstract: |
    \thispagestyle{firststyle}
    I present *Ultra Terminum*. *Ultra Terminum* (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing $O(c^3)$, $O(c^4)$, and $O(n)$ scaling configurations. UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources. UT breaks the core conflict of *Buterin's Trilemma*.
    \par
    With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin) and $O(c^3)$ scaling (the intended initial configuration), UT has capacity for 1 to 100 million tps without *layer 2* scaling methods.
    \par
    Some existing $O(c^2)$ blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1 million tps. For comparison's sake, $O(c^3)$ UT configurations with similar parameters have a raw maximum capacity of between 1 and 20 billion tps. $10^9$ tps implies a system-wide maximum throughput around 230 GB/s of raw transactions.
    \par
    Depending on parameters, the capacity of $O(c^4)$ UT ranges between $10^8$ and $10^{15}$ tps.
    \par
    Capacity of the $O(n)$ configuration is *independent* of parameters and is *unbounded* -- i.e., practically infinite.
    \par
    Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$.
