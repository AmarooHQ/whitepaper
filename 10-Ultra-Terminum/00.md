abstract: |
    \thispagestyle{firststyle}
    I/we present *Ultra Terminum*. *Ultra Terminum* (UT) is a hybrid PoW/PoS consensus method providing $O(c^2)$, $O(c^3)$, $O(c^4)$, and $O(n)$ scaling configurations. It breaks the core conflict of *Buterin's Trilemma*.
    \par
    With reasonable parameters (i.e. a per-node load comparable to Bitcoin) and $O(c^3)$ scaling, UT can comfortably exceed 1 million tps without additional scaling methods (e.g. payment channels).
    \par
    For comparison's sake, less reasonable parameters that allow existing $O(c^2)$ blockchain designs to reach 1m tps can, under $O(c^3)$ UT, provide a raw maximum capacity of between 1 and 20 billion tps (give or take). That implies a maximum throughput in the order of 500 gigabytes/sec of raw transactions.
    \par
    In the $O(c^4)$ configuration with less reasonable parameters, UT's maximum throughput is on the order of $10^{15}$ tps.
    \par
    In the $O(n)$ configuration, UT's maximum throughput is unbounded -- i.e. practically infinite.
    \par
