This document details \emph{Proof of Reflection} (PoR) and \emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT).
PoR is a new consensus primitive that is used in various ways to construct a network of cooperating blockchains.
There are many possible configurations of such a network.
Those configurations, collectively, comprise \emph{Ultra Terminum}.

\textbf{PoR:}
PoR is a method for blockchains to use other (potentially separate) blockchains as a source of additional security.
PoR can be mutual --- two blockchains can use one another simultaneously, which mutually secures both chains.

\textbf{Simplexes:}
Blockchains using PoR can use it with more than one other chain, too.
If a group of $N$ blockchains each use mutual PoR with the rest of the group, the resulting collection of blockchains is called a \emph{simplex}.
This configuration is notable because it maximizes transaction throughput, and confirms transactions faster than other blockchain designs (by a factor of $N$).
Additionally, performing a doublespend attack against a single blockchain in a simplex is as difficult as performing a doublespend attack against the whole simplex.

\textbf{Tiling:}
Multiple simplexes can be networked together into a \emph{simplex tiling}.
Simplexes that do this are called \emph{simplex tiles}.
With carefully chosen parameters, simplex-tiles can be arranged so that the resulting network has practically unbounded capacity.

Thus, UT claims to solve a well-known problem in the blockchain space, usually called the \emph{scalability trilemma}.
While there are some other systems that claim to solve this problem, none are widely recognized as a solution to the trilemma and PoR / UT is distinct from each and all of them.
