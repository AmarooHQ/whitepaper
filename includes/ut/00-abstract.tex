This paper explains and constructs \emph{Amaroo's} consensus mechanism: \emph{Ultra Terminum}.

\emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy with multiple scaling configurations, and provides a practical solution to the Blockchain Trilemma (a.k.a. Scalability Trilemma).
Although \UT{} is proof-agnostic, using \emph{Proof-of-Work} as the foundational method of proof provides decisive advantages to security, scalability, and user experience.

The Trilemma takes $O(c)$ as the measure of a traditional blockchain's scalability -- $c$ being a normal computer's capacity.
UT's simplest configuration, $\UT{1}$, scales quadratically --- $O(c^2)$.
UT's other scaling configurations are: $\UT{2}$ $(O(c^3))$, $\UT{3}$ $(O(c^4))$, and $\UTinf{}$ (theoretically $O(n)$, where $n$ $\sim$ size of the network).
\par
\emph{Proof of Reflection}, a new consensus primitive, is the key to UT's performance and security.
Proof-of-Work configurations of UT meet \emph{or exceed} the security of traditional PoW blockchains.
% are secure against 51\% attacks.

\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin, and one level of nested dapp-chains), a conservative estimate of capacity --- using Amaroo's intended launch configuration ($\UT{2}$) --- exceeds 350K TPS without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods.
% In the $\UT{2}$ configuration, doubling the block time quadruples TPS.
\par
Capacity of $\UTinf{}$ is \emph{independent} of those parameters and is theoretically \emph{unbounded} by the architecture.
It is combined with the previous variants to produce $\UTinf{1}$, $\UTinf{2}$, and $\UTinf{3}$.
\par
% Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$.
Confirmation times within UT are $O(c^{-1})$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$.
The initial configuration will have a \emph{maximum} confirmation frequency exceeding $\sim 10$ Hz.
Typically, sharded blockchains have a confirmation frequency $< 0.2$ Hz.
\par
Some existing sharded (or otherwise $O(c^2)$) blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1M TPS.
By comparison, $\UT{2}$ configurations --- with \emph{equivalent} parameters --- have a maximum capacity exceeding 10B TPS.
Such a configuration would have a system-wide maximum throughput exceeding 2000 GB/s of transaction data, and a confirmation frequency exceeding 400 Hz.
UT out-scales the architectures of Cardano, Eth2, Polkadot, and Solana by orders of magnitude.
