
I present \emph{Amaroo's} consensus mechanism: \emph{Ultra Terminum}. \emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing $\UT{1}\;(O(c^2))$, $\UT{2}\;(O(c^3))$, $\UT{3}\;(O(c^4))$, and $\UTinf{}\;(O(n))$ scaling configurations. UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources. UT is a \emph{silver bullet} that breaks the core conflict of the \emph{Scalability Trilemma} (herein called \emph{Buterin's Trilemma}).
\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin: $k = 3000$ B/s), 15s block times, and one level of nesting: \emph{Amaroo} using $\UT{2}$ will have capacity in excess of 1 M TPS without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods. In the $\UT{2}$ configuration, doubling the block time quadruples TPS.
\par
Depending on parameters, the capacity of $\UT{3}$ ranges between $10^8$ and $10^{15}$ TPS.
\par
Capacity of $\UTinf{}$ is \emph{independent} of parameters and is \emph{unbounded} -- i.e., practically infinite. It has variants $\UTinf{1}$, $\UTinf{2}$, and $\UTinf{3}$; which correspond to $\UT{1}$, $\UT{2}$, and $\UT{3}$, respectively.
\par
Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$. It is expected that the initial configuration ($k = 3000$ B/s; one level of nesting) will have a maximum average confirmation frequency of 46.9 Hz.
\par
Some existing $O(c^2)$ blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1 million TPS. For comparison's sake, $\UT{2}$ configurations -- with equivalent parameters -- have a maximum capacity exceeding 1 billion TPS. These parameters imply a system-wide maximum throughput exceeding 230 GB/s of raw transactions, and a confirmation frequency exceeding 200 Hz.
\par
