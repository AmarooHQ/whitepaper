This paper explains and constructs \emph{Amaroo's} consensus mechanism: \emph{Ultra Terminum}.

\emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing multiple scaling configurations: $\UT{1}\;(O(c^2))$, $\UT{2}\;(O(c^3))$, $\UT{3}\;(O(c^4))$, and $\UTinf{}\;(O(n))$.
UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources.
UT is built on a new consensus primitive, \emph{Proof of Reflection}, and addresses the core conflict of the \emph{Scalability Trilemma} (herein called \emph{Buterin's Trilemma}).
% \par
% UT is built on a new consensus primitive: \emph{Proof of Reflection} (PoR) -- a way for blockchains to mutually secure each other.
\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin; and one level of nested dapp-chains), a conservative estimate of capacity -- using Amaroo's \emph{initial configuration} ($\UT{2}$) -- exceeds 350K TPS without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods.
% In the $\UT{2}$ configuration, doubling the block time quadruples TPS.
\par
Capacity of $\UTinf{}$ is \emph{independent} of those parameters and is \emph{unbounded}.
It has variants $\UTinf{1}$, $\UTinf{2}$, and $\UTinf{3}$; corresponding to $\UT{1}$, $\UT{2}$, and $\UT{3}$, respectively.
\par
% Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$.
Confirmation times within UT are $O(c^{-1})$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$.
The initial configuration will have an \emph{maximum} confirmation frequency exceeding $\sim 10$ Hz.
Typically, sharded blockchains have a confirmation frequency $< 0.2$ Hz.
\par
Some existing sharded (or otherwise $O(c^2)$) blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1M TPS.
For comparison's sake, $\UT{2}$ configurations -- with \emph{equivalent} parameters -- have a maximum capacity exceeding 10B TPS.
Such a configuration would have a system-wide maximum throughput exceeding 2000 GB/s of raw transactions, and a confirmation frequency exceeding 400 Hz.
It is reasonable to say that UT out-scales each of the architectures of Cardano, Eth2, and Polkadot by multiple orders of magnitude.
