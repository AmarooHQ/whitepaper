\emph{Amaroo's} consensus mechanism: \emph{Ultra Terminum}. \emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing $\UT{1}\;(O(c^2))$, $\UT{2}\;(O(c^3))$, $\UT{3}\;(O(c^4))$, and $\UTinf{}\;(O(n))$ scaling configurations. UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources. UT is a \emph{silver bullet} that breaks the core conflict of the \emph{Scalability Trilemma} (herein called \emph{Buterin's Trilemma}).
\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin: $k = 3000$ B/s, 15s block times, and one level of nested dapp-chains) \emph{Amaroo's} capacity -- using $\UT{2}$ -- will exceed $10^6$ TPS without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods. In the $\UT{2}$ configuration, doubling the block time quadruples TPS. This is Amaroo's \emph{initial configuration}.
\par
Capacity of $\UTinf{}$ is \emph{independent} of parameters and is \emph{unbounded} -- i.e., practically infinite. It has variants $\UTinf{1}$, $\UTinf{2}$, and $\UTinf{3}$; corresponding to $\UT{1}$, $\UT{2}$, and $\UT{3}$, respectively.
\par
Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$. The initial configuration will have an \emph{maximum} confirmation frequency exceeding 90 Hz.
\par
Some existing sharded (or otherwise $O(c^2)$) blockchain designs claim to be capable of $10^6$ TPS. For comparison's sake, $\UT{2}$ configurations -- with \emph{equivalent} parameters -- have a maximum capacity exceeding $10^{10}$ TPS. These parameters imply a system-wide maximum throughput exceeding 2000 GB/s of raw transactions, and a confirmation frequency exceeding 2000 Hz. It is reasonable to say that UT out-scales each of Cardano, Eth2, and Polkadot by multiple orders of magnitude.
