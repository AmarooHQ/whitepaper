
I present \emph{Amaroo's} consensus mechanism: \emph{Ultra Terminum}. \emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing $O(c^2)$, $O(c^3)$, $O(c^4)$, and $O(n)$ scaling configurations. UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources. UT is a \emph{silver bullet} that breaks the core conflict of \emph{Buterin's Trilemma}.
\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin -- $k = 3000$ B/s; 15s block times) and one level of nesting (the $O(c^3)$ configuration), UT has capacity for between 1.7 M and 3.1 M TPS without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods. In this configuration, halving the block time quadruples TPS.
\par
Depending on parameters, the capacity of $O(c^4)$ UT ranges between $10^8$ and $10^{15}$ TPS.
\par
Capacity of the $O(n)$ configuration is \emph{independent} of parameters and is \emph{unbounded} -- i.e., practically infinite.
\par
Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$, i.e., confirmation rate is $O(c)$. It is expected that the initial configuration ($k = 3000$ B/s; one level of nesting) will have a maximum average confirmation frequency of 46.9 Hz.
\par
Some existing $O(c^2)$ blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1 million TPS. For comparison's sake, $O(c^3)$ UT configurations -- with equivalent parameters -- have a maximum capacity over 1 billion TPS. These parameters imply a system-wide maximum throughput in the 100s of GB/s of raw transactions, and a confirmation frequency of 200-800 Hz.
\par
