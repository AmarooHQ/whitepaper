In \Cref{sec:confirmation-times} we saw that \UT{}'s confirmation rate was $O(c)$, and thus the expected confirmation \emph{time} was $O(c^{-1})$.
This is roughly how long a user needs to wait for a transaction to be considered \emph{confirmed}.

In those calculations, we did \emph{not} account for the time \emph{between} the initial \emph{broadcast} of a transaction and its \emph{inclusion} in a block.
To account for the duration of the whole the transaction's journey, we need to consider: broadcast latency, the block period of the local chain, and the confirmation rate of the simplex as a whole.
$$
\text{Time-to-}x\text{-confirmations} = \phi + \frac{1}{2 B_f} + \frac{x-1}{N_1 B_f}
$$
