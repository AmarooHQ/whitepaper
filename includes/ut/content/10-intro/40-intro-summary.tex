\subsection{Paper Roadmap}

The goal of this consensus whitepaper is to introduce the novel consensus strategy forming the foundation of the Amaroo network and constructing the Simplex and Simplex Tiling.
The whitepaper is presented as follows.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \autoref{sec:proof-of-reflection} introduces the primitives and builds Proof of Reflection.
  \item \autoref{sec:constructing-ut} shows how Proof of Reflection is used to construct \UT{} and the varying levels of scaling strategies of $\UT{1}$, $\UT{2}$ and $\UT{3}$.
  \item \autoref{sec:practical-considerations} details the practical considerations for UT design, including the availability of blocks, confirmation times and information about the blockchain state.
  \item \autoref{sec:ut-complexity} presents the scaling complexity analysis of \UT{}.
  \item \autoref{sec:tiling} introduces the fundamental concepts to bring \UT{} to $O(n)$ horizontal scalability.
  \item \autoref{sec:attacks} provides information on how \UT{} is resistant to attacks.
  \item \autoref{sec:conclusions} presents the final conclusions.
\end{itemize}

Additional analysis and results from the simulations can be found in the appendices or accompanying documentation.
