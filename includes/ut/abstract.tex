
I present \emph{Ultra Terminum}. \emph{Ultra Terminum} (UT) is a proof-agnostic cross-chain consensus strategy providing $O(c^2)$, $O(c^3)$, $O(c^4)$, and $O(n)$ scaling configurations. UT remains secure against an attacker with up to $O(n)$ resources. UT is a \emph{silver bullet} that breaks the core conflict of \emph{Buterin's Trilemma}.
\par
UT is far more than the sum of its parts. UT uses both PoW and PoS chains, and, by way of construction: sound PoW chains are secure and functional even if an attacker has >50\% of that chain's hash-rate; and sound PoS chains do not suffer from \emph{Nothing at Stake}, bribe attacks, or long-term attacks. Whether a chain is soundly constructed or not depends on conditions and protocols specified in this paper.
\par
With reasonable parameters (i.e., a per-chain load comparable to Bitcoin) and $O(c^3)$ scaling (the intended initial configuration), UT has capacity for 1 to 100 million tps without \emph{layer 2} scaling methods.
\par
Some existing $O(c^2)$ blockchain designs claim to be capable of 1 million tps. For comparison's sake, $O(c^3)$ UT configurations with similar parameters have a raw maximum capacity of between 1 and 20 billion tps. $10^9$ tps implies a system-wide maximum throughput around 230 GB/s of raw transactions.
\par
Depending on parameters, the capacity of $O(c^4)$ UT ranges between $10^8$ and $10^{15}$ tps.
\par
Capacity of the $O(n)$ configuration is \emph{independent} of parameters and is \emph{unbounded} -- i.e., practically infinite.
\par
Confirmation times within UT are $\frac{1}{O(c)}$.
