{\Huge{\textbf{Status of this draft}}}
\par

Currently this paper sits at the ``Draft 1'' stage; some sections are incomplete (where the ``todos'' are).

I have left those sections currently incomplete because they require parallel and ongoing revision and validation.

In particular, further validation that Proof of Stake can be done securely \emph{at all} -- at least insofar as a \emph{blockchain} is concerned; DLTs are different and have weaker security requirements. This may not entirely align with some things I have written because I believed (and still do believe) that some Proof of Stake configurations may be \emph{possible} to do securely \emph{within} the hybrid security context that \emph{Ultra Terminum} provides.

\emph{However}, \textbf{actually determining} whether PoS can \emph{ever} be done securely (including via UT's context) is significant work. It is a milestone that we are pursuing.

My plan is to incrementally refactor the incomplete sections, which includes the following:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Include methods by which dapp-chains are possible to do securely within a \emph{purely Proof of Work} UT context. I have devised a method for this.
    \item Removing, altering, or rewriting the sections that mention \emph{Proof of Stake} to accurately represent the state-of-the-art and emphasize that UT doesn't require PoS for any of its scaling configurations.
    \item Provide quantitative analysis of UT's security -- essentially this means: backing up the claim that attacking 1 of UT's chains is as difficult as attacking \emph{all} of UT's chains.
\end{itemize}

With regards to the last item in that list: although I am confident that UT does have these security properties, confidence can be misleading. Rigorous quantitative analysis is not so fallible, and producing it will tease out any crucial design choices.

Thus our focus is currently a near-comprehensive simulation of UT (and pre-existing PoW systems) to serve as the basis for the quantitative analysis. Work on this aspect is progressing smoothly.

--- \emph{Max}, June 2\textsuperscript{nd}
